# Snapdeal Listing and Content Guidelines

## Source status
Based on Listing FAQs V1.2 [S007, historical, c. 2018 to 2020] and a Listing Content Guidelines deck [S008, undated, 2022 folder]. Category-specific guidelines live in the Seller Panel (Seller Help, Listing Guideline) and were not reviewed [S007, S008]. **Verify current rules before quoting specifics.**

## Listing types [S007]
- **Single listing:** one SKU at a time.
- **Bulk listing:** Lite content sheet, Bulk content sheet, Import listing (Excel only; no csv).
- **Sell an existing product (multi mapping):** search by name or SUPC; "Start Selling" is allowed only if brand authorization is approved for that brand, subcategory and category.

## Content fields and attributes
- Attributes are Mandatory (needed to upload), Important (add value) or Optional; values are chosen from dropdowns where provided; multi-select is allowed for some fields [S008].
- Bulk sheet product tab mandatory fields include brand, product name, description, weight, length, width, height; costing tab includes MRP, selling price, inventory and SLA; electronics may need EAN or UPC [S007].
- Some product names are auto-generated for certain product types [S007].
- Content fields, description and images must match each other [S008].

## Images [S007]
Image guideline list (historical): 850 x 995 pixels; under 10 MB; not pixelated, blurred, distorted, scanned or morphed; reflect the product and colour as it is; no logos, MRP or watermark. Accepted bulk URLs mentioned: Google Drive, paid photo bucket, paid Dropbox. **Image 1 is the landing image.** Verify current specs.

## Content that is not accepted [S008]
- Snapdeal policy text in images or content (money-back guarantee, return or refund policy, shipping or free shipping).
- Seller promotion ("best seller", "top seller", "5 star", "beware of fake sellers", "exclusive").
- Product promotion claims such as "high selling" or "best selling".
- Discount or offer text in images (also see rejections below).

## Size charts (apparel) [S008]
Create at listing under the Attributes tab ("Add Size chart"); a size chart ID is generated for reuse. Saved measurements cannot be edited but new size measurements can be added.

## Groups and variants [S007]
"Create Group" combines variant SKUs (colour, size) on one product page; unrelated SKUs need separate groups.

## Tracking and timing [S007]
Statuses: Under Review, Approved, Rejected, Drafts. The FAQ states that 85 percent of listings go live within 4 hours when compliant; it is historical, not a guarantee. If track details do not appear after bulk upload, the FAQ advises waiting 30 minutes and raising a query from the panel.

## Common rejection reasons [S007, S008]
Image: colour variation, missing front or combo view, unwanted text, watermark, QR code, MRP, discount or offer text, poor quality (blur, crop, lighting, folded view, props), discrepancy between images, mannequin shoot (where not allowed). Content: validation error (blank mandatory field), incorrect filter values, incorrect category mapping, brand mismatch, unclear or disallowed combo, banned or restricted product.

## Correction and resubmission [S007]
Open Rejected in Track Listings, read the reason and suggestion, fix content or images, resubmit.

## How the assistant should use this
Give plain-language reminders (match title, images and attributes; no promotional text on images). For exact dimensions, versions or category rules, direct the seller to the current guideline in the Seller Panel (`sd_limits_024`).
