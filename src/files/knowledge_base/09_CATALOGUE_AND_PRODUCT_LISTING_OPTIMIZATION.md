# Catalogue and Product Listing Optimization

## Overview
Catalogue quality decides whether shoppers can find, trust and buy a product. This document is **general best practice [G001]**. It is not Snapdeal policy. Snapdeal's verified listing rules are in `sd_listing_021`; where they conflict with advice here, Snapdeal's rules win.

## Why it matters
Weak listings reduce search relevance (discoverability), shopper confidence and conversion, and raise returns because expectations are wrong. See `visibility_010` and `conversion_011`.

## Titles
- Include product type, brand (only the true brand), key attribute (size, colour, model, material) in natural order.
- Avoid stuffing, unrelated brand names and promotional claims. Snapdeal's policy treats unrelated brand names and keywords as search manipulation [S009].
- Keep consistent with images and attributes.

## Descriptions and specifications
- Lead with what the product is and who it fits.
- State dimensions, material, what is in the box, care instructions.
- Be specific; avoid vague claims like "premium quality" without facts.

## Attributes
Complete every attribute available for the category. Attributes drive filters and matching. Wrong attribute values cause rejections and mismatch complaints [S007].

## Images
- Show the real product in true colour, multiple angles, scale or context.
- Avoid text overlays and watermarks (Snapdeal rejects these; see `sd_listing_021`).

## Category selection
Choose the correct category and product type; wrong mapping leads to poor discoverability and listing rejection [S007].

## Variants
Group true variants (colour, size, model) under one product page when the platform allows; unrelated products should not be grouped [S007]. Each variant needs accurate stock.

## Size and measurement information
Provide size charts and exact measurements. Apparel and decor returns often come from missing measurements (`apparel_017`, `homedecor_018`).

## Information consistency
Title, description, images, attributes and packaging must agree. Inconsistency harms trust and triggers rejections.

## Common listing quality problems
Blurry or cropped images; missing size information; generic titles; wrong category; mismatched colour; incomplete compatibility details (accessories); copied descriptions.

## Diagnostic: poor listing performance
**Problem:** A listing gets little traffic or orders.
**Possible causes:** weak title relevance, wrong category, poor main image, missing attributes.
**Evidence required:** impressions, clicks, conversion (not available), listing content (ask seller).
**Questions:** "Can you read me the title and key attributes?" "What does the first image show?"
**Diagnosis:** Content gaps are plausible but unconfirmed without funnel data.
**Actions:** Fix title, attributes and first image on the top product; compare results over time.
**Limitations:** Cannot attribute a sales change to the edits.

## Risks
Over-claiming features; copying competitors' branded wording; editing a live listing and breaking approved content (check how edits are reviewed in the Seller Panel).
