# Promotions, Discounts and Unit Economics

## Overview
A promotion should increase **profitable** sales, not only sales. General guidance [G001]. Snapdeal-specific promotion programs are not documented in sources reviewed; verify in the Seller Panel (`sd_limits_024`).

## Sales versus profitable sales
Sales growth from a discount may reduce profit if extra units do not cover the lost margin. Always compare contribution before and after.

## Contribution after discount
Contribution per unit = price after discount - cost - fees - shipping - packaging - returns allowance.
**Illustrative:** price 500, contribution 90. A 10% discount (50) leaves 40. To keep total contribution, units must rise by 90/40 - 1 = 125%. Deep discounts need very large volume lifts.

## Break-even promotion check
Required lift = old contribution / new contribution - 1. If the required lift is implausible, change the plan.

## When promotions make sense
- Clearing slow stock with known margin room.
- Launching a new product to get first orders.
- Aligning with seasonal demand peaks.
When **not** to promote: the product is out of stock, the real issue is listing quality, ratings are poor, or margin is thin.

## Bundling, cross-selling, upselling
- **Bundle:** sell two compatible items together (for example a phone cover with tempered glass for the same model).
- **Cross-sell:** suggest related items.
- **Upsell:** suggest a better version.
Check Snapdeal's rules: combo listings are not allowed in some product types and combo details must match the images and description [S007].

## Seasonal campaigns
Prepare stock and listings before the season; do not discount constrained stock. Confirm campaign mechanics in the Seller Panel.

## Evaluating a promotion
Compare orders, ASP, contribution and cancellations in the promotion window against a similar period. Watch for a post-promotion dip.

## Diagnostic
**Problem:** Seller wants a discount to fix falling sales.
**Possible causes:** stockouts, listing, visibility, price; or real price gap.
**Evidence required:** ASP, stockout, margin, competitor price.
**Questions:** "What margin do you keep per unit?" "Is the issue views or purchases?"
**Actions:** Fix root cause first; if a discount is chosen, cap depth and duration.
**Limitations:** Margin data unavailable.

## Misrepresentation caution
Never inflate MRP to show a larger discount; Snapdeal's policy treats MRP misrepresentation as prohibited [S009, historical]. See `sd_policy_023`.
