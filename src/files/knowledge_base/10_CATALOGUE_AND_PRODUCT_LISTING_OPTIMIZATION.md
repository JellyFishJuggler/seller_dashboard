# Catalogue and Product Listing Optimization

A product listing is the seller's shop window. This document covers general e-commerce listing best practices: titles, descriptions, attributes, categorisation, images, variants and content accuracy. These are general practices, not Snapdeal requirements. Snapdeal's actual mandatory fields, image rules and category attributes are category-specific and must be taken from current official documentation or the panel (see KB-24).

## 1. What listing quality affects

Listing quality influences whether a product is found (KB-11), whether it is clicked, whether the buyer trusts it (KB-12) and whether the buyer keeps it (KB-15). Content problems often show up as high clicks but few orders, or as high returns for "not as described".

## 2. Product titles

A good title is accurate, readable and includes what a buyer would search for.

General pattern: Brand (if any) + Product type + key attribute(s) + size/model/compatibility + pack quantity.

Examples (illustrative):
- Weak: "Best Quality Cable Fast Charger New".
- Better: "USB Type-C to Type-C Fast Charging Cable 1 m, Braided, Pack of 1".
- Weak: "Beautiful Kurti".
- Better: "Women's Cotton Straight Kurti, Printed, Round Neck, Regular Fit".

Avoid keyword stuffing, misleading claims, competitor brand names you do not sell, and all-caps shouting. Title rules (length, format) vary by platform; confirm them.

## 3. Product descriptions

Describe what the buyer needs to decide: what it is, what it is made of, dimensions, what is included, usage, care, compatibility, warranty (if real). Use short paragraphs or bullet points. State limitations honestly (for example "fits phones up to 6.5 inches"). Do not copy competitors' text; do not exaggerate.

## 4. Attributes and categorisation

Attributes are structured fields: colour, size, material, brand, model, compatibility, weight, capacity. Complete, accurate attributes improve filtering, search and buyer confidence. Wrong category placement hides a product from relevant browsing and can cause content or compliance issues. The official Snapdeal documentation shows attributes are fetched per category, so the required attributes differ by category (KB-24).

## 5. Product images

- Show the actual product, from several angles, on a clean background.
- Include a scale or in-use image for size-sensitive products.
- Show colour accurately; avoid heavy filters.
- Include images of what comes in the box.
- Show close-ups of material, stitching, ports, finish.
- Image technical requirements (size, count, background) are platform-specific; confirm.

## 6. Variants: size, colour, model

Variants should be linked under one product where the platform allows, each with its own accurate stock and price. Problems: wrong size labels, missing size charts, mixing different models in one variant list, colour photos that do not match the stock sent. Stock and sales should be monitored per variant (see KB-18 for apparel).

## 7. Content accuracy

Accurate content reduces returns. Check: measured dimensions, true colour, material composition, real compatibility, actual included items, truthful claims. Be able to prove claims (certifications, warranty).

## 8. Listing completeness checklist

1. Category correct.
2. Title clear and accurate.
3. All mandatory attributes filled.
4. At least several quality images (platform rules apply).
5. Description with dimensions, material, usage.
6. Variants correct.
7. Price and stock correct.
8. Warranty, authenticity and return information consistent.
9. Packaging details and what is in the box.

## 9. Common catalogue mistakes

- Titles with no product type or key attributes.
- Missing or inconsistent size information.
- Low-quality or misleading images.
- Wrong or missing compatibility information.
- Copy-pasting listings from different products.
- Incorrect category or attributes.
- Variant stock not matched to reality.
- Outdated or mismatched content after a supplier or product change.
- Claims that cannot be proven.

## 10. Diagnosing a listing problem

- Low impressions: title, category, attributes, stock availability (KB-11).
- High impressions, low clicks: title, main image, price (KB-11).
- Good clicks, few orders: content mismatch, price, reviews, delivery promise (KB-12).
- High returns: description or image mismatch (KB-15, KB-16).

Check changes in listing content before the sales change.

## 11. Trade-offs

More detailed content takes time and can reveal weaknesses (such as small size), but it lowers returns. Fancy lifestyle images may raise clicks but mislead. A balance of attractive and accurate is best.

## 12. Questions a KAM should ask

- Did you edit the title, images or description recently?
- Who created your images, and do they show the real product?
- Which attributes are filled for your top products?
- What are customers asking or complaining about in reviews or returns?
- Do your size or compatibility details match what you actually ship?

## 13. What not to conclude

- Do not claim a listing change will raise ranking or sales by a stated amount.
- Do not describe Snapdeal's listing rules as fact unless sourced (KB-24).
- Do not assume a listing is the problem without impressions, clicks and conversion evidence.
