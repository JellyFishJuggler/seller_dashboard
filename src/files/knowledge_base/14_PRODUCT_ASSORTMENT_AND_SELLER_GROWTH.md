# Product Assortment and Seller Growth

## Overview
Assortment is the set of products a seller offers. Good assortment balances proven sellers with controlled experiments. General guidance [G001].

## Portfolio analysis
Rank products by: revenue contribution, historical orders, stock availability, margin (if known), return/complaint level (if known). Prototype data covers historical orders for stocked-out products and bestseller flags only. Ask the seller for the rest.

## Bestsellers and concentration risk
If a few products carry most of the business, a stockout, quality issue or listing problem on one product can cut sales sharply. Diversify gradually; do not abandon the core.

## Long-tail products
Many low-volume products can add variety but cost cash and attention. Review them periodically; stop reordering items that do not move.

## Prioritizing products for attention
1. Bestsellers out of stock (fix supply).
2. Bestsellers with weak content or complaints.
3. Products with decent history but low availability.
4. Slow movers consuming cash.

## New product introduction
- Start with adjacent items (compatible accessories, matching decor, coordinated apparel).
- Small first batch; observe orders and returns.
- Complete listing quality before launch.
No sales predictions without evidence.

## Product lifecycle
Intro, growth, maturity, decline. Mature products need stock discipline; declining ones need exit plans.

## Seasonal assortment
Plan seasonal products early (see category playbooks). Avoid leftover seasonal stock by buying conservatively.

## Category expansion
Expand only where the seller has sourcing strength and can meet Snapdeal's brand and listing requirements (brand authorization may be needed to sell some existing listings [S007]).

## Growth planning
Pick one growth lever per quarter: stock availability, catalogue upgrade, new adjacent products. Track orders, ASP, cancellations (`actionplan_016`).

## Diagnostic: concentration
**Problem:** A few products make most sales.
**Questions:** "Which products give most orders?" "Do you have alternatives?"
**Actions:** Protect top-product stock; add one adjacent product; improve second-tier listings.
**Limitations:** Share-of-sales by product not in tools.
