# Snapdeal Seller Policies and Risk Guidelines

## ⚠ Status warning
Main source: Snapdeal's **Prohibited Seller Activities and Consequences Policy, version 1.2 dated 01-07-2017** [S009]. It is the latest version seen and is **historical; current status unverified**. It forms part of the Seller Agreement, and the Seller Agreement prevails if they conflict [S009]. Never present these as confirmed current rules; say "Snapdeal's policy document from 2017 says..." and point to the Seller Panel. This is not legal advice.

## Rules (paraphrased) [all S009, v1.2, 2017]

### Counterfeit and brand misuse
- **Issue:** listing fake products, imitation brands, or using a brand name or logo in title, description or images without being the manufacturer or authorized.
- **Why it matters:** IP infringement, buyer harm, listing removal or account action.
- **Preventive action:** list only genuine goods under the correct brand; keep invoices and authorization papers.

### Brand authorization and compliance documents
- **Issue:** missing or fake invoices and brand authorization certificates; the policy refers to "BRAVO" compliance documents (term not explained in the document).
- **Preventive action:** keep valid documents and ask Snapdeal support what is required for your brand and category.

### MRP and pricing
- **Issue:** selling above MRP, charging extra taxes over MRP, displaying a wrong MRP, hiding MRP or offers.
- **Why:** consumer harm and policy breach.
- **Preventive action:** display the true printed MRP and price at or below it.

### Not-for-sale, expired, near-expiry goods
- **Issue:** promotional samples, freebies, expired products, items past 75 percent or more of shelf life.
- **Preventive action:** do not list such stock.

### Warranty
- **Issue:** offering a warranty not applicable in India.
- **Preventive action:** state only valid, India-applicable warranty.

### Banned or regulated products
- **Issue:** selling banned products or regulated products without license (examples: hazardous materials, narcotics, prescription medicines).
- **Preventive action:** check the Banned Products list in Seller Help.

### Rating or order manipulation
- **Issue:** buying your own listings, or sales, purchases, cancellations or returns used to manipulate ratings, restrict competition or create abusive arbitrage.
- **Preventive action:** no self-purchases or coordinated activity.

### Seller identity and multiple seller codes
- **Issue:** fake or invalid identity documents (PAN, GSTIN, bank details); operating several seller codes without disclosure.
- **Preventive action:** keep documents valid and disclose related seller codes.

### Wrong or inferior product
- **Issue:** inferior quality, wrong item, used item or empty parcel (if reasonably proven).
- **Preventive action:** quality check and accurate packing.

### Listing conduct
- **Misleading title:** titles must describe only the actual item; no bait and switch, irrelevant words.
- **Search manipulation:** unrelated brand names or keywords, hidden text; may lead to listing removal or suspension, sometimes without prior warning.
- **Links:** listings cannot point to outside websites or off-platform sales.
- **Fee circumvention:** no off-platform offers or inflated shipping.
- **Profanity** is prohibited; delist offending products.
- **Display name:** must not breach trademarks or be an email or URL.

### Platform interference
Automated tools such as crawlers or bots to pull data, or any interference with the website, are prohibited; only seller-panel areas may be edited.

### Packaging
Only Snapdeal standard wrapping materials; no competitor or non-standard packaging.

### Consequences
Possible loss recovery from the seller, listing blocks, panel access suspension, termination, payment withholding during investigation, and limits on contesting (the policy mentions a 7-day window to represent after levy of damages). Amounts are not stated.

## Regulatory context
Indian consumer-protection rules for e-commerce apply to sellers (disclosure, accurate advertising, grievance officer) per secondary summaries [S011]. Reports of September 2026 amendments exist but are **unverified** [S012]. Direct sellers to official sources or professional advice.

## Use in conversation
Use these only to warn gently ("This could risk your listing"); never allege wrongdoing.
