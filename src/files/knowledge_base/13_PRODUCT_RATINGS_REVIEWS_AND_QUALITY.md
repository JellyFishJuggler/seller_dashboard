# Product Ratings, Reviews and Quality

## Overview
Ratings and reviews influence buyer confidence and conversion. General guidance [G001]. Customer review data is **not available** to the prototype (`data_avail_025`). Ask the seller to describe or paste recent reviews.

## Why ratings matter
Shoppers use ratings as a proxy for quality and reliability. A low average or a cluster of similar complaints can reduce purchases. Check both average and count (`metrics_004`).

## Classifying negative feedback
Use categories:
1. **Product quality:** defects, durability, performance.
2. **Mismatch:** colour, size, features differ from listing.
3. **Packaging/damage:** arrived broken, loose, poorly packed.
4. **Delivery/service:** late, wrong item, missing parts.
5. **Compatibility/fit:** (accessories, apparel).
Count each category to find repeated complaints.

## Diagnostic: repeated customer complaints
**Problem:** Ratings dropping or a product has recurring complaints.
**Possible causes:** supplier batch quality, listing mismatch, packaging, unclear size or compatibility info.
**Evidence required:** review text, return reasons, order timing.
**Questions:** "What are the top two complaints?" "Did they start after a new supplier or batch?"
**Actions:** Fix listing information, packaging or supplier issue; update expectations in the description.
**Limitations:** Review data not in tools; do not speculate.

## Review-based improvements
- Mismatch: update photos, measurements, colour notes.
- Quality: quality check before dispatch; talk to supplier.
- Packaging: stronger materials per Snapdeal packaging rules (see `fulfilment_008`).

## Ethical and compliant feedback management
- Respond to genuine issues and fix root causes.
- **Never** buy, write or incentivize fake reviews; never ask buyers to remove legitimate criticism or suppress genuine reviews.
- Snapdeal's prohibited-activities policy lists manipulating one's own or others' ratings, including through sales, purchases, cancellations or returns, as prohibited [S009, historical].
- India's IS 19000:2022 sets principles for collecting, moderating and publishing online reviews; it was introduced as voluntary in 2022 and later reports mention moves toward making it mandatory; current legal status unverified [S013].

## Reputation management steps
1. Collect complaints weekly.
2. Tag categories.
3. Fix the top cause.
4. Deliver the best possible experience to prompt natural reviews.

## Risks
Chasing ratings with prohibited tactics can lead to listing removal or account action (see `sd_policy_023`).
