# Home Decor Seller Playbook

## Overview
Home decor depends on size, colour, finish and safe delivery. Buyers cannot touch the item, so listing accuracy and packaging decide satisfaction. General guidance [G001]. Check Snapdeal category guidelines in the Seller Panel for category-specific rules (`sd_limits_024`).

## Product dimensions
Give exact length, width, height (and diameter or thickness when relevant) in centimetres, with the unit stated. Add a size reference in an image (for example next to a common object). Missing dimensions cause "too small or too big" returns.

## Material and finish
Specify material (wood type, metal, resin, ceramic, fabric blend) and finish (matte, glossy, textured). Avoid terms that imply a material you do not use.

## Colour representation
Lighting alters colour. Use neutral lighting, photograph the real item, note that slight variation may occur. Mismatched colour is a common complaint.

## Fragile product handling and packaging
Glass, ceramic and wall art frames break in transit. Use protective inner packing and sturdy outer cartons, and mark fragile. Snapdeal's historical policy limits packaging to Snapdeal standard wrapping materials [S009]; confirm what is currently required for fragile categories in the Seller Panel.

## Photography and room context
Include a clean main image plus a room-context image that shows scale. Check that Snapdeal's image rules allow the image type (text or watermark overlays are generally disallowed [S007, S008]).

## Variants
Colours, sizes and sets should be clear variants with correct stock. Do not group unrelated designs.

## Seasonal demand
Festive and wedding seasons and home-moving periods raise decor demand in many markets. Check the seller's own last-year pattern before buying stock.

## Inventory planning
Decor items can be slow movers; keep modest stock on long-tail designs and protect bestsellers.

## Buyer expectation mismatches
**Problem:** Returns or poor ratings due to "not as expected".
**Possible causes:** size misjudged, colour different, material feel, breakage.
**Evidence required:** return reasons and review text (not available).
**Questions:** "What do buyers say: size, colour or damage?"
**Actions:** Add dimensions and context photo, improve packaging, clarify finish.
**Limitations:** No review or return data in tools.

## Example (illustrative)
A seller of resin wall clocks sees returns. Adding a clear diameter and a photo on a wall beside a door frame reduces size doubt; reinforced corner packing addresses breakage. Effects are not guaranteed.
