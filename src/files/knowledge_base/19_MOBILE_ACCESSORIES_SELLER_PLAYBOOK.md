# Mobile Accessories Seller Playbook

## Overview
Covers, tempered glass, chargers, cables, earphones and similar items. The main risks are compatibility errors, authenticity claims and packaging. General guidance [G001]; Snapdeal order documentation mentions serial number or IMEI requirements for certain products [S003].

## Device compatibility
- List the exact phone model(s), not just the brand (for example "for [Brand Model X]").
- Variants by model must be separate or clearly grouped; a wrong-model variant is a top return cause.
- Check that the cutout and size match the model.

## Technical specifications
For chargers and cables state: connector type (for example USB-C, Lightning, micro-USB), power rating, cable length, supported fast-charging standards, materials. Do not claim a standard the product does not support.

## Connector and port compatibility
Buyers often misread connector types. Show the connector in an image and name it in the title.

## Variants
Colour and model variants need accurate stock per model. A stockout on a popular model hurts a whole product family.

## Warranty
State warranty period and who provides it. Snapdeal's historical policy prohibits selling with a warranty that is not applicable in India [S009, historical]. Confirm current warranty fields in the listing.

## Authenticity
Do not use brand names or logos you are not authorized to use. Counterfeit and brand misuse are prohibited [S009, historical]. Selling an existing branded listing may require brand authorization [S007]. Keep purchase invoices.

## Serial or IMEI details
Snapdeal's order documentation says IMEI is needed for products such as mobile phones and serial numbers for some computer accessories before label printing [S003]. Check whether your product type requires them.

## Packaging
Use protective packaging; screen protectors and cables are easily damaged. Follow Snapdeal packaging rules (`fulfilment_008`).

## Returns caused by compatibility mismatch
**Problem:** Returns say "does not fit" or "not compatible".
**Possible causes:** vague title, grouped models, wrong attribute.
**Evidence required:** return reasons and model mentioned by buyers (not available).
**Questions:** "Which models are returned most?" "Is the model name in the title?"
**Actions:** Put the exact model in the title and attributes; split variants by model; add a compatibility line.
**Limitations:** Return data not in tools.

## Assortment and demand planning
New phone launches create demand for matching covers and glass; older models decline. Review model-wise sales and avoid overstocking old models. Quantities need seller sales history and lead times (`inventory_007`).

## Diagnostic: top cover out of stock
**Questions:** "Which model is it for?" "Restock lead time?" Prioritize models with the highest historical orders.
