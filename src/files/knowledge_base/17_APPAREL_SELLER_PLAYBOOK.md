# Apparel Seller Playbook

## Overview
Apparel is variant-heavy (size by colour) and returns often come from fit and colour expectations. General guidance [G001]; Snapdeal size chart and photo rules from [S008] and [S007] (verify currency).

## Size and fit issues
- Brands and fabrics fit differently; "M" is not universal.
- Provide an exact size chart with garment measurements (chest, length, shoulder, waist as relevant).
- Snapdeal's guidelines describe creating a size chart during listing and reusing its size chart ID; existing measurements cannot be edited, but new sizes can be added [S008]. Confirm in the Seller Panel.

## Colour and variant management
- Group size and colour variants of the same design on one page (Create Group) [S007].
- Images must reflect true colour; colour variation across views causes rejection [S007].
- Keep per-variant stock accurate.

## Product photography
Snapdeal's content guidelines flag model shoot quality, background, pose, blur, mannequin shoots and flat or table-top shoots as common rejection issues, and say text on images is not allowed [S008, S007]. Exact category rules differ; check the category guideline in the Seller Panel.

## Fabric and material descriptions
State fabric composition, stretch, transparency, wash care. Avoid vague words without facts.

## Returns due to sizing or mismatch
**Problem:** Returns rising on a garment.
**Possible causes:** size chart missing or wrong, colour shown differently, fabric feel mismatch.
**Evidence required:** return reasons (not available), size distribution of returns.
**Questions:** "Which sizes return most?" "What do buyers say about fit?"
**Actions:** Add measurements, correct colour photos, note fit ("runs small").
**Limitations:** No return data in tools.

## Inventory across variants
Stockout often hits specific sizes (S, M, L). One missing popular size can hurt a design. Track stock per size; keep buffer on the most-ordered sizes.

## Seasonality
Apparel demand shifts with weather, festivals and weddings. Plan stock early using last year's same-period orders if the seller has them. Avoid overbuying seasonal designs.

## Fashion assortment
Maintain a core of basics and a small set of trend items. Review slow designs often.

## Bestseller replenishment
Restock best designs in the sizes with most history first. Do not set quantities without lead time and sell-through information (`inventory_007`).

## Diagnostic: bestseller kurti out of stock
**Evidence:** tool shows high past orders and out-of-stock status.
**Questions:** "Which sizes sold most?" "Lead time from your manufacturer?"
**Actions:** Prioritize restock for top sizes; ensure listing remains accurate meanwhile.
