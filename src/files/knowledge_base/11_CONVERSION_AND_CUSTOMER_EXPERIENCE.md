# Conversion and Customer Experience

## Overview
Conversion is the share of interested shoppers who buy. It depends on page quality, price, trust, availability and delivery expectations. General guidance [G001]. Conversion rate is **not available** in the prototype (`data_avail_025`).

## Funnel
Impressions, clicks, product page visits, orders, deliveries, retained orders (not returned). Each step has a different fix. Define the conversion denominator before discussing it (`metrics_004`).

## Factors that influence conversion
- **Product page:** clear title, complete specifications, size or compatibility data.
- **Images:** true colour, multiple angles, scale.
- **Price:** relative to similar listings and perceived value.
- **Trust:** ratings, count of ratings, accurate descriptions (`reviews_013`).
- **Delivery expectations:** promised delivery time, cash-on-delivery availability, return terms as shown on the platform.
- **Availability:** unavailable variants lose the sale.

## Diagnostic: high impressions, low clicks
**Problem:** Product is shown a lot but rarely opened.
**Possible causes:** weak main image, unclear title, price visible in results looks high, irrelevant placement.
**Evidence required:** impressions and clicks (not available), title and main image (ask), price vs similar items.
**Questions:** "What does the first image show?" "Does the title say what the item is?"
**Diagnosis:** Likely a presentation problem, but only measurable with funnel data.
**Actions:** Refresh the main image and title for one top product.
**Limitations:** Cannot measure CTR now.

## Diagnostic: high clicks, low orders
**Problem:** Shoppers open the page but do not buy.
**Possible causes:** price gap, missing size or compatibility info, poor reviews, long delivery expectation, out-of-stock variants.
**Questions:** "Are all sizes or models in stock?" "What do recent buyers complain about?"
**Actions:** Fix information gaps and availability first; consider a small, margin-checked price test (`pricing_006`).

## Diagnostic: orders increasing while returns increase
**Problem:** Growth with more returns.
**Possible causes:** mismatch between listing and product, quality issues, sizing, compatibility, packaging damage.
**Evidence required:** return reasons (not available).
**Questions:** "What reasons do buyers give?"
**Actions:** Tighten descriptions and measurements; check quality and packaging.
**Limitations:** Without reason codes this is a hypothesis.

## Diagnostic: product interest without completed purchases
**Problem:** Wishlist or cart activity without orders (if the seller sees it), or inquiries without buying.
**Possible causes:** price hesitation, delivery uncertainty, trust gaps.
**Actions:** Clarify delivery and return information shown on the page; verify what Snapdeal allows in content (policy text such as shipping or refund claims is not permitted in images or content per [S008]).

## Conversion experiments
Change one element at a time (main image, title, price within margin). Run for a defined period, keep stock available, compare orders per visit if available. Do not claim causality from a single test.

## Risks
Chasing conversion with discounts can lower profit (`promotions_012`).
